TANSHIN PIPELINE WORKING RULES

1. Never manually edit a generated or exemplar Markdown report.
   Report changes must be made in the generic pipeline, prompts, schemas,
   normalization, validation, or rendering code and then produced by a normal
   pipeline run or offline reprocessing.
   This restriction does not apply to source documentation such as README.md
   or AGENTS.md when documentation itself is being maintained.

2. Preserve historical runs. Do not improve a report by patching its rendered
   output or by overwriting historical artifacts outside the pipeline's normal
   archival process.

   The default report root is final_output/. Canonical Markdown filenames end
   in the local run date as YYYYMMDD. When reports are archived under a
   timestamped history directory, preserve those dated filenames unchanged.

3. Judge report quality primarily through direct qualitative review against the
   exemplars and prior runs. Deterministic comparison scores are diagnostic
   metadata only and must not be treated as the authoritative quality judgment.

4. Keep all changes company-agnostic. Do not add company-specific report fixes.

5. Never initiate a Gemini or OpenAI API request from automated development
   work. Every API-backed run must be started manually by the user.

6. English narrative prose renders yen-denominated statement amounts at or above
   ¥1 billion in one-decimal billion notation, smaller amounts in million
   notation, and per-share amounts as forms such as ¥95 per share. Preserve
   economic value within display precision, scale, sign, percentages, margins,
   payout ratios, and statement type; never perform foreign-exchange conversion.
   Current synthesis and translation responses do not contain citation
   bookkeeping. Do not request or persist a synthesis/translation citation
   graph of source IDs, quotations, page references, evidence records, or
   evidence ledgers. Research-map page locations remain compact navigation
   aids, not report citations.

7. Keep model names as source constants in tanshin_pipeline/config.py. The .env
   file is credentials-only and may contain GEMINI_API_KEY, GEMINI_API_KEY2,
   and OPENAI_API_KEY.

8. A Japanese report uses a PDF-backed research request and a separate
    PDF-backed, research-map-guided synthesis request. Optional English
    translation is a third request. The default profile uses gemini-3.6-flash
    with GEMINI_API_KEY for
    every stage. The key2-translation profile changes only the translation
    credential to GEMINI_API_KEY2. When consecutive stages share a Gemini
    credential, preserve a 75-second inter-stage cooldown whenever their
    combined estimated input and maximum-output allowance reaches 225,000
    tokens, leaving 10% headroom below the 250,000-token planning limit.
    Research is coverage-first: every selected filing receives one chronological
    memo, while Python derives comparable annual forecast/result metrics before
    synthesis. Request 1 extracts filing-specific information and does not rank
    business drivers, decide longitudinal themes, score management, or draft
    final conclusions. Do not recreate source-ID graphs, commentary taxonomies,
    or citation-ready quotations in the research response.
    It may organize a small number of material capital-allocation tracks into
    cross-filing factual lifecycle records. Capital absorbed or released,
    immediate transaction/accounting effects, and subsequent economic returns
    must remain separate. A track may represent persistent organic capital
    accumulation without a discrete announced decision. Collectively, the records
    should reveal the directional allocation mix across businesses without
    inventing segment invested-capital amounts or ROIC. These records must not
    decide whether value was created.
    Require direct per-filing observations covering operating results, financial
    condition, forward-looking information, strategy and execution, segment and
    business drivers, capital allocation, and material footnotes. Compress
    comparable annual forecast-result pairs into one financial anchor per
    year-end filing so qualitative management discussion is retained without
    broad historical table duplication. Treat management commentary as a claim
    or explanation to test against reported results, financial condition, cash
    flow, mandatory footnotes, and later filings, not as proof of success.
    Reported capital stocks and operating assets are primary evidence for
    capital-allocation analysis.
    Request 2 receives the map and the selected PDFs. The map is an attention
    guide rather than a source boundary; PDFs remain authoritative for context,
    counterevidence, and final conclusions.
    Request 2 acts as an independent investor analyst. Favorable or repeated
    management language cannot substitute for observable outcomes.
    Management-linked attribution is weaker than destination-level evidence;
    aggregate ROE, EPS, BVPS, profit, dividends, or buybacks cannot by themselves
    prove that a particular capital destination created value.

9. For a default, primary-key-only run, explain that generation should be free
    when GEMINI_API_KEY is eligible for Gemini's free tier. Any displayed yen
    amount is a conservative paid-tier upper-bound estimate.

10. Deterministic validation is intentionally non-gating. Run it and preserve
    all diagnostic artifacts, but do not let valid, publishable, error, warning,
    or quality-score results suppress canonical Markdown for a response that
    can be parsed, normalized, and rendered. Manual review is the publication
    authority. API, structured-output parsing, missing-artifact, processing, and
    rendering failures may still stop the pipeline.
    Research-map cross-checks follow the same policy: a Pydantic-parseable map
    proceeds to synthesis while findings remain diagnostic artifacts.
